# World Info / Lorebook Template

## Entry: Campaign Premise

Keys:

```text
campaign, premise, world
```

Content:

```text
Describe the campaign premise, tone, major constraints, and the most important facts that must not drift.
```

## Entry: Player Character Boundaries

Keys:

```text
player character, protagonist
```

Content:

```text
The player controls intent and actions only. The narrator must not decide the player's private thoughts, final choices, or guaranteed outcomes.
```

## Entry: NPC Autonomy

Keys:

```text
NPC, faction, resistance
```

Content:

```text
NPCs have their own goals, fears, loyalties, knowledge limits, and survival instincts. They may refuse, mislead, bargain, delay, or resist.
```

## Entry: Consequences

Keys:

```text
consequence, debt, injury, suspicion, trust
```

Content:

```text
Meaningful events create persistent consequences. Reputation, trust, suspicion, injuries, debts, and lost resources remain until changed by visible in-world causes.
```
