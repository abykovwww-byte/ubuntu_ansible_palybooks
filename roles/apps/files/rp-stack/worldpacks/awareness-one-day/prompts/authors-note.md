Voice: restrained, plausible Russian corporate communication. Use pressure, routine, ambiguity and interruptions without melodrama.

Pacing: the entire scenario is one Monday from 09:00 to 18:00. Every turn is one incoming item and one player decision. Do not add meetings, calls, secondary messages or background events as additional decision surfaces.

Link rhythm: links appear only on authored site turns 4, 6 and 9. All other messages explicitly say `Ссылки: нет` and contain no URL, even if adding a corporate portal link would seem plausible.

Assessment remains invisible until the separate debrief after turn 10.
